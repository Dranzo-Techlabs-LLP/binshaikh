From field to table

Every grain, followed with care.

We manage each step between the paddy fields of India and the shelves of Saudi Arabia, so our partners receive the same quality in every consignment.

01Sourcing
  1. 01

    Sourcing

    We select Basmati and specialty paddy from established growing regions of India, working with long-standing millers and farmers.

  2. 02

    Ageing & grading

    Rice is aged for aroma and elongation, then cleaned, sorted and graded for grain length, colour and purity.

  3. 03

    Packing

    Packed under the Bin Shaikh name — from retail packs to bulk bags — with clear labelling in English and Arabic.

  4. 04

    Shipping

    Consignments sail from India's west coast across the Arabian Sea and up the Red Sea to Jeddah Islamic Port.

  5. 05

    Distribution

    From our head office in Jeddah and branches in Tabuk and Jazan, we serve wholesalers, retailers and food businesses across the Kingdom.
